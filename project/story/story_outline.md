# RAI — Story Outline (DRAFT v0: concept only, no timings)

> Chapter boundaries, durations and sync points are **deliberately blank**. They will be
> derived from `music_analysis.json` once the song is analysed (brief §4–5). This
> draft only fixes what each chapter means and how Rai and the flower relate.

Throughline: Rai's lightning and the flower are one system seen from two sides, energy
and structure. The film is not "lightning destroys flower". Chaos passes *through*
order and leaves a more complex order behind.

| # | Chapter | Rai | Flower state | Relationship | Light |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AWAKENING | Unseen, then eyes only | Bud, then A (perfect symmetry) | The flower exists before her; its core pulse is the first "heartbeat" | One spark in total dark |
| 2 | EXISTENCE | First full reveal, still and composed | A, then B (slight asymmetry) | The field leans toward her; her breath bends symmetry by a hair | Cool rim light; first faint static in her hair |
| 3 | DISTORTION | Restless; hair rising, ribbons lifting | C, then D (distortion, bifurcation) | Her rising charge pulls petals off-axis; they split, imitating branching lightning | Flicker; violet appears where blue meets magenta |
| 4 | EXPLOSION | Release: arms open, full discharge | E, then F (vortex, chaos) | The flower becomes the vortex her lightning flows through. Not destroyed, but turbulent | Peak white; the only full-frame flash in the film |
| 5 | FRAGILITY | Close and quiet; eyes wet, hair falling still | F, settling: drifting debris strands | The strands float around her like embers; one strand touches her shoulder tattoo | Near-dark; single soft key light; breathing space |
| 6 | TRANSCENDENCE | Eyes open, serene power | F, then G emerging | Robe print and tattoo light up and lift off. Rai's lightning *draws* the new symmetry | Violet dominant; order and chaos side by side in frame |
| 7 | REBIRTH | Gaze down at the new flower, faint smile | G, a new fold count, residual turbulence at the rim | A new flower stays when she fades: its core holds a tiny blue spark (hers) | Calm; the last frame mirrors the first spark |

Mirrored bookend: the film opens on a blue spark becoming a flower and closes on a flower holding a blue spark.
